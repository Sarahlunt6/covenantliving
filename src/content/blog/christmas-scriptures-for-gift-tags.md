A gift tag is a small thing, but it's often the first thing someone reads. Adding a scripture turns "To" and "From" into a quiet reminder of why we give gifts at all.

The trick is finding verses short enough to fit and meaningful enough to matter. Here are twelve favorites, grouped by the feeling you want to share.

## The birth of the Savior

These are perfect for family, neighbors, or anyone you want to point back to the manger.

> For unto you is born this day in the city of David a Saviour, which is Christ the Lord.
>
> — Luke 2:11

> And they came with haste, and found Mary, and Joseph, and the babe lying in a manger.
>
> — Luke 2:16

> But Mary kept all these things, and pondered them in her heart.
>
> — Luke 2:19

## Peace and joy

Lovely for teachers, coworkers, and friends going through a hard season.

> Glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace, good will toward men.
>
> — Luke 2:14

> Make a joyful noise unto the LORD, all the earth: make a loud noise, and rejoice, and sing praise.
>
> — Psalm 98:4

> And my spirit hath rejoiced in God my Saviour.
>
> — Luke 1:47

## Light

Beautiful on a candle, a lamp, or any gift for someone who needs hope.

> And the light shineth in darkness; and the darkness comprehended it not.
>
> — John 1:5

> Arise, shine; for thy light is come, and the glory of the LORD is risen upon thee.
>
> — Isaiah 60:1

> When they saw the star, they rejoiced with exceeding great joy.
>
> — Matthew 2:10

## Love, faith, and gratitude

Sweet for a spouse, a child, or a thank-you gift.

> We love him, because he first loved us.
>
> — 1 John 4:19

> For with God nothing shall be impossible.
>
> — Luke 1:37

> Thanks be unto God for his unspeakable gift.
>
> — 2 Corinthians 9:15

## Matching the verse to the person

| Giving to | Try |
| --- | --- |
| Neighbors and ministering families | Luke 2:11 or Luke 2:14 |
| A missionary or someone far from home | Luke 1:37 |
| A new mom | Luke 2:19 |
| Teachers and Primary leaders | Isaiah 60:1 |
| Someone who's grieving | John 1:5 |
| Your spouse | 1 John 4:19 |

## Tips for writing them

- **Write the reference smaller** than the verse so the words stand out.
- **Use a fine-point pen** if you're writing by hand. Long verses need room.
- **Keep a list** of who got which verse, so you can choose a new one next year.
- **Add a line of your own** on the back: "This verse made me think of you because…"

However you share them, a scripture on a gift tag is a small way to keep the Savior at the center of a busy, wrapping-paper-covered season.
