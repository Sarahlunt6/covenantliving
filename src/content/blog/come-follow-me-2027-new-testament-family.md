In 2027, *Come, Follow Me* turns to the New Testament. That means a whole year walking with the Savior through the Gospels, the early Church, and the letters of the Apostles. The first week, December 28 to January 3, starts with Matthew 1 and Luke 1, so the year begins with the story of His birth.

If you've ever started January strong and fizzled out by March, you're not alone. A little preparation in December makes all the difference.

## Talk about your hopes before you start

Before the first lesson, sit down together and talk about what you want this year to look like. A few questions to get you started:

- What do we hope to learn about Jesus Christ this year?
- Is there a scripture we want to memorize as a family?
- When and where will we study? (Be realistic. Ten minutes at breakfast beats an hour you never get to.)
- How will we help each other keep going?

Write the answers down somewhere you'll see them again. In December 2027, you'll love reading what you hoped for.

## A simple weekly rhythm

You don't need a perfect lesson every day. A rhythm like this works for most families:

| Day | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Sunday | Read the week's chapters (or a few highlights with little ones) |
| Monday–Saturday | Read a little each day and check it off |
| Midweek | Share one thing you learned about Jesus Christ |
| End of week | Write down a verse you loved and any questions you have |

Little ones can draw a picture of the story instead of writing. A sketch of a manger or a boat on the Sea of Galilee is a perfect journal entry.

## Dates worth marking now

- **Easter is March 28, 2027.** Plan to read the accounts of the Savior's last week and Resurrection in the days leading up to it.
- **General Conference** usually lands on the first weekend of April and October. Expect to adjust those weeks.
- **The last week of the year** returns to the Christmas story, so the year ends where it began.

## Keep a record

The families who stick with it all year usually have one thing in common: they write something down. It doesn't have to be much. A verse, a question, a sentence about what someone shared. Over 52 weeks, those small notes become a record of your family learning about the Savior together.

> And Jesus increased in wisdom and stature, and in favour with God and man.
>
> — Luke 2:52

That's a good goal for all of us this year.
