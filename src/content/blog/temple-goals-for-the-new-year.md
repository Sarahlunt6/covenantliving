A new year is a natural time to think about the temple. Maybe you want to go more often. Maybe you want each visit to mean more. Maybe you just want to get back in the habit. Whatever brought you here, a simple goal can help.

## Choose a goal that fits your life

The best temple goal is one you'll actually keep. Your circumstances are different from anyone else's: how far you live from a temple, your work schedule, your season of life with little ones. Be honest about what's realistic.

| If you… | Try a goal like… |
| --- | --- |
| Live close to a temple | Once a week, or every Saturday morning |
| Live an hour or more away | Once a month, on a set day |
| Have young children at home | Alternate with your spouse, or trade babysitting with a friend |
| Are just getting back into it | One visit this month, then build from there |
| Are a youth with a limited-use recommend | A regular trip with your family or ward for baptisms |

Write your goal down. A goal you can see is much easier to keep.

## Pick a regular time

Goals turn into habits when they have a time attached. "I'll go to the temple more" is a wish. "I'll go the first Saturday of every month" is a plan. Put it on the calendar now, before the new year fills up.

## Bring a name

Few things make a temple visit more meaningful than serving someone from your own family. Spend a few minutes on FamilySearch before you go and look for ancestors who need ordinances. Even one name can change the whole feel of a visit.

## Track your visits

Coloring in a box after each visit sounds small, but it's surprisingly motivating. Watching a year of visits add up helps you keep going on the weeks when it's hard to get there.

## Remember the day

Before you leave the temple parking lot, take two minutes to write down:

- The date and which temple
- Whose names you served for
- Any impression or answer you received

Those notes become a record of how the Lord has blessed you through temple worship. On hard days, you'll be grateful you wrote them down.

## Look back at the end of the year

Next December, sit down with your notes. How many times did you go? Which visits stand out? How has temple worship changed you this year? Then set your goal for the year after.
